Nothing goes straight to automated. There is a ladder.

The question is never whether a process can be automated. It is which rung it is on today, and what it takes to earn the next one. Every rung pays for itself, so nobody has to bet the programme on the top.

Why almost nothing reaches rung seven. The process changes before you get there, and people are in the decision culturally or legally. Their judgement is part of what the decision is.

The decision log is a by-product of climbing. By the time you want rung five, you already have the trail that lets a regulator agree to it.

Encore: the pitch that isn’t there

A party trick from my PhD. Switch the harmonics of a 200 Hz tone on and off, then play what’s left.

What’s in the sound

With harmonics 3 to 6 on there is no energy at 200 Hz, yet the waveform still repeats every 5 ms and most people hear a 200 Hz pitch. Keep any two neighbouring harmonics and the pitch usually stays at 200 Hz. Leave just one and you hear that harmonic’s own pitch instead.
