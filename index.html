How I work

From an ongoing fractional role to a fixed-scope build.

  • If you can’t measure it, don’t do it.
  • Only build a proof of concept with a clear path to production.

Fractional CTO or Head of AIOngoing

I set the technical strategy, make the architecture calls, hire the first AI team and support the board and investors.

DiscoveryOne to three weeks

I map how the process really works, where AI pays and where it doesn’t, and say so when a problem isn’t agentic.

Proof to productionFour to eight weeks

A working system, judged on your own data or replayed history rather than a demo, and built to your regulator’s constraints from day one.

From auditory neuroscience to production AI

PhD in auditory neuroscience at the University of Cambridge on an MRC scholarship, after an MA in Natural Sciences there. I’ve written software for 13 years, starting with the signal processing and stimulus code for that research.

Harmonics 3 to 6 of 200 Hz: 600, 800, 1,000 and 1,200 Hz. There is no energy at 200 Hz, yet the pattern repeats every 5 ms and most people hear a 200 Hz pitch. My research looked at how we hear pitch in sounds like this, from single neurons to listeners.
